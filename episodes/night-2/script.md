# BUNKER ANNIE
## Day 2 — The Women Behind the Bars

**[SCENE 1 — PRISON CORRIDOR — NIGHT]**

It was 1:10 in the morning.

The prison was almost completely dark. The electricity was gone. The emergency generators had failed. And somewhere beyond the locked security doors, something was moving.

Annie stood in the corridor, holding her flashlight.

Thirty years working in corrections had taught her one thing. When everything goes wrong, people look to whoever is still standing.

And tonight, that person was Annie.

Behind the bars of the women's housing unit, frightened voices called out.

"Officer Annie! What's happening? Why aren't the lights coming back on?"

Annie approached the cells.

"Listen to me, ladies. I need everybody to remain calm. We have a serious situation. Communications are down. We have no power, and right now, I cannot tell you when help is coming."

One woman shouted, "Are we being attacked?"

Annie hesitated.

"I don't know exactly what we're dealing with. But some people inside this facility have become extremely violent. They're not responding to commands, and they're attacking anyone they encounter."

The corridor fell silent.

Then another woman whispered, "You mean they're zombies?"

Annie looked toward the darkness.

"I didn't say that. But whatever is happening, we need to survive it."

**[SCENE 2 — THE WOMEN'S HOUSING UNIT]**

Annie moved slowly along the cells, shining her flashlight across the faces of the women she'd supervised for years.

Some were crying. Some were praying. Others were watching her closely.

"Now, I need you to understand something. I'm not opening these cells tonight. Not until I know what's safe on the other side of these doors."

A woman named Denise stepped forward.

"Then what are we supposed to do? Sit here until we starve?"

"No," Annie replied. "We're going to get organized."

She took a small notebook from her pocket.

"I need volunteers. Women who can remain calm and follow instructions. We're going to establish teams."

She looked toward Denise.

"You're going to help me coordinate the women on this tier. I need a count of everyone who's here, anyone who's injured, and anyone who needs medication."

Denise nodded.

"What about food?"

"That's our next priority. I need two women on each tier to keep track of whatever food and drinking water you already have. Don't share supplies between cells yet. Just tell me what you've got."

Another inmate called out.

"And if those things get in here?"

Annie raised her flashlight.

"Then we keep these security doors closed, we stay quiet, and we don't give them a reason to come looking."

**[SCENE 3 — ANNIE'S PLAN]**

Annie found an empty desk near the security station.

She opened her notebook and began writing.

*Women accounted for. Medical needs. Food and water. Security.*

There was something else she needed to consider.

If the prison remained without power, the women couldn't stay locked inside indefinitely.

Someone would eventually have to reach the food storage areas. Someone would have to find medical supplies. And someone would have to discover whether the outside world was still functioning.

But Annie had another problem.

Her home was less than ten miles away.

And inside that house was her cat.

She pictured him sitting beside his empty food bowl, waiting for her to come home.

She had left for work expecting an ordinary shift.

Now she had no idea whether she'd ever see him again.

Annie closed her eyes.

"I can't leave him there," she whispered.

But then she looked toward the women's housing unit.

And she knew she couldn't abandon them either.

**[SCENE 4 — A DIFFICULT DECISION]**

Annie returned to the cells.

"Ladies, I need you to listen carefully. We may be here for several days. Maybe longer. We need to start thinking about what happens if nobody comes to rescue us."

Denise gripped the bars.

"Are you leaving us, Annie?"

Annie looked at her.

"I haven't decided anything yet."

"Because if you leave, we're trapped in here."

"I know."

For the first time that night, Annie didn't have an answer.

She had spent most of her adult life protecting people inside these walls.

But she also had a life beyond them.

And somewhere in the darkness, something was changing.

A distant metallic crash echoed through the corridor.

Every woman stopped talking.

Annie turned toward the sound.

Then came another noise.

A slow, dragging scrape against the floor.

Annie lowered her voice.

"Everybody, away from the bars. Now."

**[SCENE 5 — THE CLIFFHANGER]**

She switched off her flashlight.

The corridor disappeared into darkness.

For several seconds, nobody moved.

Then a voice came from somewhere beyond the security door.

"Annie?"

It was faint.

Almost familiar.

"Annie... let me in."

Her heart began pounding.

She recognized that voice.

But she also remembered what she'd seen happen to Walt.

And she knew that hearing a familiar voice didn't necessarily mean the person behind it was still the same.

Annie slowly raised her flashlight toward the door.

She reached for her notebook and wrote one final sentence.

*Do not open the door until you know who's on the other side.*

Then something struck the metal.

Once.

Twice.

And a third time.
