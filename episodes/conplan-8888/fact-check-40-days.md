# Fact check — the "40 days" in CONPLAN 8888-11

**Source:**
- Wikisource transcription of the USSTRATCOM FOIA release: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/CONPLAN_8888-11_%22Counter-Zombie_Dominance%22
- Original PDF: https://www.stratcom.mil/Portals/8/Documents/FOIA/CONPLAN_8888-11.pdf
- Page numbers are the PDF's pages.
- Full text saved in `research/conplan-8888-11-text.md`.

## Context that must be kept in the video
1. **It's a training exercise.** The document is a fictional scenario written by "junior military officers undergoing training" (p.1); the transmittal letter says it's "intended for use only during JOPP-related training" (p.3). It is not a real order or a real prediction.
2. **30 vs 40.** The phasing table says lock down for **30 days**. The written Phase 3 orders say shelter mission-essential personnel for **at least 40 days**, and the recon teams go out **no earlier than 40 days**. The script states both ("a table says thirty… the orders say at least forty").
3. **Scope.** The 40-day provision covers **USSTRATCOM forces and their mission-essential personnel**, not every military base and not civilians.
4. **Civilians at the gate aren't mentioned.** The plan never orders turning civilians away. Elsewhere it tasks forces to "provide security to civilian areas, evacuation zones" (p.11). The script says outright that the closed gate is **our fiction**.
5. **Where "40 days" comes from.** It's the planners' assumption that infected humans fail within 30–40 days without food and water (pp.14, 30).

## What we can say on screen (accurate)
- "In 2011, officers training at U.S. Strategic Command wrote a plan for a zombie apocalypse: CONPLAN 8888. It was a training exercise."
- "A table says lock down for thirty days. The orders say: shelter all mission essential personnel in place for at least forty days."
- "After at least forty days, reports are broadcast in clear text on unsecured comms, so isolated survivors can find them."

## What we must NOT say
- ❌ "The Pentagon's secret plan", "the military will lock you out", or "every base locks down for 40 days".
- ❌ Anything implying the plan is real policy, or that real bases would turn civilians away.
- ❌ Real base names as the setting. The story uses the fictional **Calder Air Station**. A web search (25 Nov 2026) found no real installation of that name; the nearest are unrelated (Cavalier AFS, Calumet AFS, Caswell AFS).
