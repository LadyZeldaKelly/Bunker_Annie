Water safety (the survival steps in the story)
• At least 1 gallon of water per person per day (Ready.gov): https://www.ready.gov/water
• CDC, water in an emergency: https://www.cdc.gov/water-emergency/about/
• About half a gallon a day just for drinking (Mississippi State University Extension): https://extension.msstate.edu/publications/disaster-relief-drinking-water-emergency
• 8 drops of unscented 6% bleach per gallon of clear water, wait 30 minutes; let cloudy water settle and filter it first (EPA): https://www.epa.gov/ground-water-and-drinking-water/emergency-disinfection-drinking-water

REAL VS FICTION
CONPLAN 8888-11 is a real U.S. Strategic Command training exercise. Lines marked REAL FILE are quoted or closely paraphrased from it. Calder Air Station, Jo, Eli and every event in this story are fiction.
